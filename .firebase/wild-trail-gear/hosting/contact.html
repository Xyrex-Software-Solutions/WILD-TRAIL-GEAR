FAQ

COMMON Questions

How do I rent gear?
Just message us on WhatsApp with the gear you need and your dates. We'll confirm availability and pricing right away.
What's the minimum rental period?
We have a 2-day minimum for most items. Bundles require at least 3 days.
Can I pick up the gear?
Yes! Collect from our location in Panadura. We can also discuss delivery for large orders.
What if gear is damaged?
Normal wear is expected. We assess damage fairly and keep charges transparent — always discussed before any deduction.
Do you offer last-minute rentals?
We do our best! Message us on WhatsApp and we'll check availability immediately.
Is payment in LKR?
Yes, all prices are in Sri Lankan Rupees. We accept cash on pickup and selected digital payments.
